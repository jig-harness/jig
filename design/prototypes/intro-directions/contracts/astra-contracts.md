# Introduction page direction contracts

All four use the opening, five steps and close from brief.md in order, with verbatim copy. Each figure caption reuses its step label. The hero uses the opening headline as its figure label. Local fonts use their host licences, with no font files distributed. Each direction uses the paired tokens in light and dark themes and reserves focus for keyboard focus. All five steps and their figures remain readable without JavaScript.

## A1 · Neatline

1. **Name** — Neatline.
2. **Idea** — The reader moves down a map legend while the machinery stays at a fixed point of reference.
3. **Typefaces** — Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia, then serif, under their installed system licences.
4. **Scale** — The opening runs at 38–64px. Step titles use 26px roman and labels use 16px italic.
5. **Layout** — The hero pairs the opening with the whole line. Below it, five entries scroll beside a sticky plate. At 320–390px the plate sticks above the entries and occupies one quarter of the viewport height.
6. **Shapes** — Fine rectangular borders carry short edge ticks. Matching ticks align each numeral with its entry.
7. **Colour** — Use bg around a card plate, ink for names, line-strong for ticks and accent for the active location.
8. **Imagery** — Hold the engraving at one scale across steps. At step three, a locator brackets the two slots. Set the caption along the lower border.
9. **Devices** — Print coordinates 01–05 beside the entries. Underline the shipped clause once and the planned clause with dashes. A double bracket locates the person's merge or answer.
10. **Motion** — The locator slides between positions. Reduced motion places it immediately. Without scripting, each entry includes its own located still.
11. **Buyer** — A lead can trace each rule to the part it governs.
12. **Risk** — A shallow phone plate can obscure fine hatching.

## A2 · Barline

1. **Name** — Barline.
2. **Idea** — A single vertical index counts five bars while aligned rows show who acts and when.
3. **Typefaces** — Use ui-monospace throughout, carried by `SF` Mono, Consolas or the system monospace face under its installed licence.
4. **Scale** — Use 32–48px medium opening type, 24px step titles and 16px body. Upright tabular digits align down the page.
5. **Layout** — A persistent left rail links to five bars. Each bar spans two rows, with prose above and its engraving below. The hero introduces that rhythm. At phone widths the rail contracts to a column of digits.
6. **Shapes** — Horizontal rules meet the rail at upright barlines. A heavier closing stroke ends the fifth bar.
7. **Colour** — Set ink on bg. Use line for the horizontal rules, accent for the current bar and sunk behind its number.
8. **Imagery** — Fit the whole hero scene across the lower row. Enlarge the slot assembly within step three's row. Align the caption with the first prose character.
9. **Devices** — Bar numbers 1–5 double as anchor links. A broken underline marks the planned clause. Paired vertical strokes enclose the person's merge or answer. Agent actions take single strokes.
10. **Motion** — On entering step two, a timing stroke sweeps across the figure once. Reduced motion shows its endpoint.
11. **Buyer** — The rail lets an engineer return directly to the account limit.
12. **Risk** — Long monospaced titles take several lines at 320px.

## A3 · Fieldbook

1. **Name** — Fieldbook.
2. **Idea** — Each entry begins with an observation in the engraving and leaves room beside it for the operating rule.
3. **Typefaces** — Use Georgia italic for headings and system-ui for notes, carried by Segoe UI or San Francisco under their installed licences.
4. **Scale** — Keep the opening at 22px and step headings at 18px regular italic. Body notes use 16px sans.
5. **Layout** — Five entries run down one continuous page. Each figure fills three quarters of the writing area, with its note beside the relevant detail. On phones the note follows immediately below.
6. **Shapes** — An open margin, short leaders and hanging numerals keep entries together without enclosing them.
7. **Colour** — Use bg for paper, ink for hatching, muted for captions, accent for the slot detail and hl behind human decisions.
8. **Imagery** — Give the hero engraving the full writing width. In step three, crop close enough to count both slots while keeping the wall visible. Place the caption at the leader's foot.
9. **Devices** — Small italic numerals descend the margin. A dotted enclosure holds the planned clause. A square bracket beside the highlighted words identifies the person's action in greyscale.
10. **Motion** — The step-three leader draws once from the slots to the note. Reduced motion shows the complete line.
11. **Buyer** — The engineer can inspect the physical relationship before reading its rule.
12. **Risk** — A reader scanning titles may pass over the quiet headings.

## A4 · Sheet Set

1. **Name** — Sheet Set.
2. **Idea** — Each step occupies a complete drawing sheet whose title block identifies the mechanism under review.
3. **Typefaces** — Use system-ui, carried by San Francisco, Segoe UI or the host sans face under its installed licence.
4. **Scale** — Bold 44–76px opening type establishes the cover. Step titles use 28px bold, with 14px letterspaced labels and 16px body.
5. **Layout** — The cover leads into five sheets, each at least one viewport tall. Each has a drawing field above its own title block. At 320–390px the block stacks and the sheet grows to fit.
6. **Shapes** — A ruled perimeter joins the drawing to a subdivided title block. Its narrow end cell holds the sheet number.
7. **Colour** — Use card for the sheet, sunk for its surround, ink for boundaries and accent for the mechanism under discussion.
8. **Imagery** — Centre the complete engraving on the cover. Fit step three's assembly inside generous clearance on all sides. Its caption occupies the block's first cell.
9. **Devices** — Number the sheets 1/5 through 5/5. A scalloped revision outline surrounds the planned clause. A double-bordered cell identifies the person's merge or answer.
10. **Motion** — The fifth sheet's decision cell receives one brief impression, compressing then settling. Reduced motion shows the settled cell.
11. **Buyer** — A lead can share one sheet with its rule and ownership intact.
12. **Risk** — Tall sheets make comparison across steps slower.
