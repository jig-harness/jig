# Task: write four direction contracts (issue #1066 design round)

Read `../briefs/brief.md` first: it states what is fixed and the copy. Then write four contracts of
about 200 words each, into one result file (path given by the coordinator).

## You may not open
The current site's source or its design documents (anything under a `website/` or `design/`
directory of the Jig repository, README.md, FACTORY.md). Use only `../briefs/brief.md`, `../figure/`
(to see the engraving manner), `../canvas/logo.svg` and the applicable assignment file
(`astra-assignments.md` or `claude-assignments.md`). Independence from what exists is the point.

## Each contract gets one assigned reference world and one assigned constraint
You stay inside both. Select the matching row from `astra-assignments.md` or
`claude-assignments.md`. The reference world is a source of layout, typographic
and shape ideas, never a pastiche of its content.

## Fields, in this order, for every contract
1. Name (one or two words)
2. The idea, one sentence
3. Typefaces by name with licence (system stacks only; say which system faces carry it)
4. Type scale and character of headings
5. Layout idea of the stepped page (how five steps are traversed; where the figure sits)
6. Shape language (rules, frames, key caps, numerals)
7. Colour use and neutrals (only the fixed tokens)
8. Imagery: how the isometric engraving figure is placed, scaled, captioned
9. How the recurring devices look: the step number, the "planned" mark (greyscale-proof),
   a person's moment (merge, answer) versus an agent's step
10. The one motion moment (must degrade to a still under reduced motion)
11. Why it fits the buyer (the evaluator in brief.md)
12. What it risks

## Difference in kind
No two contracts share a typeface treatment, a layout idea or a motion idea. If two could be
the same page with a different font, rewrite one. End with a comparison table of the four on
those three axes (type, layout, motion).

## Report
Write the result file, then reply with its path and a 3-line summary. Do not render anything.
