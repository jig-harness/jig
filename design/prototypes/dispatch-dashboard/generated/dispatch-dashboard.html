slotOne of an account’s concurrent-session places, held by a launch record that is pending, alive or unknown. A takeover and the record it replaces count as one. A parked drive holds none.
wallA subscription account’s usage limit, recorded once a session stops on it. The account is left out of every launch until the wall’s reset.

driftingA running stage outside its expectation: threads not falling, no pull request in time. Reported and notified, never acted on.
nudgeThe one fixed prompt the dispatcher may send by itself: “continue”, to a session waiting after a usage limit that has since reset.
