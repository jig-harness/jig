# Contract B (Astra): the rails

Read [figure-brief.md](figure-brief.md) first: it fixes the story, the files, the
words and the style. This contract fixes what is left open to you.

- **Reference world:** a harbour container terminal (gantry cranes, straddle
  carriers, rail-mounted trolleys) and a letterpress print shop (a press bed,
  rollers, a stamp).
- **Assigned constraint:** the agents move and the item moves little. The item
  rests on a long bench and slides one bay at a time. The agents travel along
  rails or tracks to it, each carrying the tool of its station. At least two
  agents must be mobile (on a rail, on tracks or on wheels) and no agent may be
  a pedestal arm fixed to the floor. The line runs near end to far end without
  closing; the side feed is its own short rail.
- **Open:** the composition, the projection (true isometric or paraline), the
  agent vocabulary, the form of each item state, the look of the person's desk.
- **Proposal first:** `vocabulary.md` is your proposal of the agent for each
  station and of the item's five forms. Then draw it.
- **Item forms:** your choice, as a family.
