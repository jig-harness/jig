# Contract C (Gemini 4 Neon): the terraces

Read [figure-brief.md](figure-brief.md) first: it fixes the story, the files, the
words and the style. This contract fixes what is left open to you.

- **Reference world:** a watchmaker's or instrument-maker's bench: tweezers, a
  loupe, a seal press, a jeweller's lathe, a stamping die, a tray of parts.
  Agents are precision instruments scaled up to machines.
- **Assigned constraint:** the line climbs. The stations stand on ascending
  terraces, queue at the bottom and the person's desk at the top, so progress
  reads as height; the line never closes. The side feed descends as a short
  chute from Follow up back to the queue.
- **Open:** the composition, the projection (true isometric or paraline), the
  agent vocabulary (instrument-maker's, mechanical), the form of each item state,
  the look of the person's desk.
- **Proposal first:** `vocabulary.md` is your proposal of the agent for each
  station and of the item's five forms. Then draw it.
- **Item forms:** your choice, as a family.
