# Contract A (Claude): the bench

Made by Claude. Drawn by the candidate's own isometric engine (`World` and
`renderWorld` in `candidates/a-claude/`) from one description.

- **Reference world:** the plan of a machine-shop bench and a catalogue of
  industrial manipulators: pedestal-mounted arms with a single fitted tool.
- **Assigned constraint:** the stations are fixed and the item travels. One
  straight run, near end to far end, the queue at the near end and the person's
  desk at the far end. Each agent is a pedestal with a jointed arm of boxes and
  one tool. The side feed is a short return rail beside the run, never joined into
  a ring.
- **Item:** a card that gains a sheet (the pull request), then comment tabs and a
  rung of status lamps, then a lamp row all lit, then a stamped, filed slab.
- **Agents (the plan, before drawing):** Pick up a gripper arm; Build a welding
  arm; Open a press; Drive an inspection probe on an arm; Repair a wrench arm;
  Follow up a small rover on the feed; the dispatcher a gantry head over gauges.
- **Person:** a green solid with a figure on it at the desk.
