# Contract D (Astra, open brief): surprise me

Read [figure-brief.md](figure-brief.md) for the **Fixed** section, the
**Storyboard**, the **words**, the **style** and the **files**. Everything else is
yours.

Draw how one work item travels through Jig's line: it enters a queue, becomes an
issue picked up by a machine, grows a pull request, collects comments and build
status, goes green, and a person merges it. Make the agents that do this work
memorable and make the item's change of form the thing a reader remembers.

You have creative permission. Choose the composition, the camera, the projection,
the number of agent kinds, the vocabulary of machines and the forms of the item.
Do not follow any layout from the style reference; follow its manner. Do not copy
the obvious. If a better figure needs a different arrangement of the stations
(not a loop, not a closed ring), draw that.

Argue your choices in `report.md`: what you chose, what you dropped, why.
