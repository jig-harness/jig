# Scene description: the terraces

## Fixed plate and layout

One stepped workshop plate, drawn in a uniform 22-degree paraline projection, carries the entire line from bottom-left to top-right so every station sits higher than the last. In the wide frame (`960 x 540`) the terraces rise gently across the width; in the tall frame (`390 x 520`) the same stepped structure is recomposed with steeper terrace risers and tighter treads so it fills the portrait frame cleanly while keeping labels at least 14 units tall.

From the single shared scene description, Steps 1, 7 and 8 frame the full terraced block (Queue, ascending terraces, descending side chute, green summit desk, and Dispatcher plinth). Steps 2 through 6 crop and zoom the camera onto the acting terrace and its immediate neighbours—letting the rest of the stair run off the frame's edges—so the item's five forms (registration ring, hinged leaf, comment tabs, check sockets, signet seal) and each mechanical agent's tool read at full scale in both wide and phone layouts.

From lowest to highest along the ascending spine:

- **Queue** sits in a recessed tray at the bottom-left foot of the terraces.
- **Threshold slot** separates the Queue from Terrace 1; it lies flush and open in Steps 1 through 7 and holds the raised **Usage limit** wall in Step 8.
- **Terrace 1 — Pick up** holds the tweezer crane beside the entry step.
- **Terrace 2 — Build** holds the jeweller's lathe.
- **Terrace 3 — Open** holds the screw clasp press.
- **Terrace 4 — Drive** holds the articulated loupe and caliper probe.
- **Terrace 5 — Repair** holds the indexing staking turret.
- **Terrace 6 (Summit) — Merge** holds the green watchmaker's desk and the person seated behind it, with a chamfered question corner facing Drive and a small finished resting pad just past the desk at the upper-right end of the line.
- **Follow up** stands on a compact side-outpost beside the summit resting pad, feeding a narrow, mostly empty **side chute** that descends along the back-left edge of the terraced block straight back to the tail of the Queue.
- **Dispatcher plinth** stands on the front-right apron beside the Queue and Terrace 1, physically separate from the ascending track. It carries the dial-reading vernier agent and four tilted read-only circular dial gauges (**Memory**, **Processor**, **Disk**, **Forge budget**) with engraved tick marks and pointer needles, and no numerals or counts.

Every still is rendered from this same full workshop scene; overview steps (1, 7, 8) frame the whole structure, while action steps (2–6) follow the work closely across the terraces.

## Step 8 — Capacity

- **Places and objects:** Full overview of the terraced block. Three Form 1 issues wait pressed inside the bottom-left **Queue** tray, the lead issue stopped flush against a single tall hatched **Usage limit** wall raised squarely across the threshold between Queue and Terrace 1, casting a long hatched shadow back over the waiting Queue. Every upper terrace (Pick up through Merge), the exit pad and the descending chute are empty.
- **Agent poses:** **Dispatcher** is in its **act** pose, its pivoting vernier reading-arm lowered over the four circular dial gauges to read them without touching the Queue or track. Pick up, Build, Open, Drive, Repair, Follow up and the person are all at **rest**.
- **Item form and position:** Form 1 (**Issue**) plates wait behind the closed **Usage limit** wall inside the **Queue**; nothing climbs the line until the limit resets.
- **Motion note:** When the usage limit resets, the single wall drops back flush into the threshold slot and Pick up resumes lifting from the Queue.
