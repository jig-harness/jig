# Vocabulary: the terraces

Stations climb watchmaker's bench terraces in one paraline projection. Mechanical agents are scaled-up instruments in ink, paper and grey, without faces or human features. Green marks only the person's desk, passing checks and the merged seal.

## Agents

- **Pick up** — Tweezer crane: counterweighted jib with twin blades lifting work from the queue.
- **Build** — Jeweller's lathe: ribbed bed, spoked flywheel and sliding tool carriage.
- **Open** — Screw clasp press: twin-pillar arch press crimping the pull-request leaf open.
- **Drive** — Loupe and caliper probe: jointed arm with optical lens and feeler needle.
- **Repair** — Staking turret: C-frame punch wheel and graver resetting a failed check.
- **Merge** — A person at the green summit desk stamping work with a signet seal.
- **Follow up** — Escapement pusher: toothed wheel and lever tipping new work onto the narrow chute.
- **Dispatcher** — Dial vernier: pivoting sight reading four bench gauges without touching the track.

## Item forms

One notched movement plate transforms across five greyscale-legible states:

1. **Issue** — Flat notched plate with three ruled lines.
2. **Pull request** — Base plate hinged to an open upper leaf.
3. **Comments and checks** — Open folio with two margin tabs and three front sockets: two hollow pending rings, one crossed failure.
4. **Checks green** — Tabs cleared; three sockets seated with green checkmarks.
5. **Merged** — Closed plate crowned by a raised scalloped green signet seal.
