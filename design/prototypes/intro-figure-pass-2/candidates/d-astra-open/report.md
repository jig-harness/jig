## Drawing choices

The upper engraving keeps each station in place while the lower detail brings the paper close enough to read the review marks. On the narrow plate the route turns once, ending at the desk. Follow up sits over the queue tail so its chute can be short. The drawings omit a physical rail from the desk to Follow up. The merge ends the main rail.

The articulated tools share a bolted casting. Their working ends differ. The speech shapes, rings, cross and ticks give the review margin a silhouette that survives a change of palette.

## Fix pass

In both formats the ink bracket sits below the acting station’s label in steps 2–6 and above the Follow up label in step 7. The overview marks the acting station and places the current ticket at its tool. During follow-up the new issue leaves the dispenser while the sealed ticket stays at Merge. The wide overview sits higher to give the working plate more room. Captions sit below its front edge. In the opening detail a slotted rack holds a row of punched issues beside the resting pickup foot.

The capacity detail places one upright wall across the queue lane. The dispatcher leans over a separate row of instruments. Memory has a vertical column, Processor a needle, Disk a divided disc and Forge budget a horizontal scale.

The follow-up detail shows a left-facing dispenser feeding the tail of the rack. A punched issue rides the chute behind a direction arrow. The overview keeps the sealed ticket at Merge.

The capacity instruments occupy the full lower row, with larger faces and captions beneath their bases. The dispatcher stands above them. A short leader connects Usage limit to the wall.
