# Scene description: the bench

World: x runs down and to the right of the frame along the line, y toward the
viewer's left, z up. The belt runs along x. Stations stand behind the belt (negative y). The side feed
runs beside the belt, in front of it. The dispatcher's rail runs along the
front strip. Units are arbitrary; the belt is 1.5 wide.

Run order along x: Queue (0.9 to 3.3), Pick up (4.6), Build (7.6), Open (10.6),
Drive (13.6), Repair (16.6), Merge desk and person (19.6). Cameras: steps 1 and 7
show the line from the queue to the desk; steps 2 to 6 and 8 follow the item or the
action at a fixed zoom. The phone frame uses the same scene with the camera
closer.

## Step 1: Queue
Five or six issue cards stand in a file tray at the belt's start. Every arm is
folded at rest, the press head is raised, the rover waits at the feed's start,
the dispatcher's cart is at rest on its rail. Nothing is on the belt.
Motion note: the Pick up arm swings over the tray and its gripper closes on the first card.

## Step 2: Pick up
The Pick up arm is in its act pose: the forearm reaches over the tray end, the
gripper has closed on the first issue and lifted it clear of the belt. One fewer
card stands in the tray. Everything else at rest.
Motion note: the card is set on the belt and travels to Build and Open.

## Step 3: Build and open
The Open press is in its act pose: the platen is down just over the issue, which
now has a second sheet slid out of it (form 2). The Build arm has settled at rest.
Motion note: the item moves on to Drive.

## Step 4: Drive
The Drive arm is in its act pose over the pull request: the lens ring is low, a
dashed scan plane lies on the sheet. The item is form 3: comment tabs on the
sheet's front edge and a rack of three lamps behind it (a ring, a dashed ring, a
crossed disc). A question card (a "?" mark on a small stand) stands on the
person's desk; the person is at rest with arms down.
Motion note: the Repair arm swings over the rack.

## Step 5: Repair, then green
The Repair arm is in its act pose: the socket is down on the rack, the lever
turned. The item is form 4: three filled discs with ticks, tabs ticked. The
question card is gone.
Motion note: the item reaches the merge desk.

## Step 6: Merge
The person's right arm reaches the key and the key is pressed down. The item is
form 5, merged: a block under a green seal with a tick, on the belt's end.
Motion note: the follow-up rover loads a new issue.

## Step 7: Follow up
The rover is on the side feed, mid-way, carrying a small new issue; chevrons mark
the feed's direction toward the queue. The merged item rests at the belt's end.
Every arm at rest.
Motion note: the rover reaches the queue's tail and the issue joins the file.

## Step 8: Capacity
The belt, the queue and the Pick up arm stand as before. A hatched wall stands
across the belt in front of Pick up, with an issue waiting in front of it. The
dispatcher's cart is out on its rail above a console of four gauges of different
shapes: Memory (three columns), Processor (a dial with a needle), Disk (stacked
platters with a read arm) and Forge budget (a tank). Dashed sight lines run from
the cart's eye to each gauge. No numerals, one wall. Only the wall stops work.
Motion note: none; the figure holds.
