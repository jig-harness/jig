# Vocabulary: the bench

Fixed stations on one straight belt; the item travels, the arms reach. One
projection (true isometric), one belt from the queue to the person's desk. Agents
are machines in ink, paper and grey. Green marks only the person and checks.

## Agents

- **Pick up**: a pedestal arm with a two-jaw gripper that lifts the first card out of the queue's file tray.
- **Build**: a pedestal arm with a welding torch; sparks mark the act pose.
- **Open**: a C-frame press; a platen comes down on the issue and presses the pull-request sheet out of it.
- **Drive**: a pedestal arm with a lens ring and a feeler; a dashed scan plane marks the act pose over the sheet.
- **Repair**: a pedestal arm with a socket and a lever, turned in the act pose.
- **Follow up**: a small tracked rover on a thin side feed that carries a new issue back toward the queue.
- **Dispatcher**: a cart on a low rail with a mast and a reading eye; dashed sight lines reach the gauges.
- **Merge**: a person on a green plinth at a desk with one key.

## Item forms (one card, changing)

1. **Issue**: a card with a clipped corner, an open ring and ruled lines.
2. **Pull request**: the card with a second sheet slid out of it, tied by a pin, with a node-and-line mark.
3. **Comments and checks**: the sheet with comment tabs on its edge and a status rack of three lamps: a ring, a dashed ring and a crossed disc.
4. **Checks green**: the tabs ticked, every lamp a filled disc with a tick.
5. **Merged**: the sheet folded into a block under a green seal with a tick.
