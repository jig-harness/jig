# Code of conduct

Jig's issues, pull requests, discussions and site are places to work on a
tool together. They work when everyone in them is treated as a colleague.

## Expected

- Be direct about the work and kind to the person. Say what is wrong with
  a change, not with its author.
- Assume the other person acted in good faith, ask before concluding, and
  accept a decision you argued against once it is made
  ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
- Keep to the subject. Jig's spaces are for Jig.

## Not accepted

Insults, harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing someone's private
information, sexual language or imagery, and sustained disruption of a
discussion after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainer enforces this page, by a warning, by hiding or deleting
content, or by removing someone from the project's spaces, in proportion
to what happened. Report a problem privately through the repository's
private report form (Security, then "Report a vulnerability", with
"Conduct" in the title), which reaches the maintainer and the
repository's administrators and nobody else
([GOVERNANCE.md § Reaching the maintainer](GOVERNANCE.md#reaching-the-maintainer));
reports are read within 3 working days and handled in confidence.

This page applies to everyone in the project's spaces, the maintainer
included.
