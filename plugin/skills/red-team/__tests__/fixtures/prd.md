# Product requirements: Shared lists

## Problem statement

- **Current situation:** People in one household keep separate shopping
  lists on their phones and buy the same things twice.
- **Desired outcome:** Everyone in a household sees one list and knows what
  has already been bought.
- **Why now:** Duplicate purchases are the most common complaint in support.

## Users and personas

### Household member

- **Goal:** Add what is needed and see what others have bought.
- **Pain today:** Finds out at home that someone else bought the same thing.
- **Success looks like:** "I never buy milk twice."

## Requirements

### FR-1: Share a list

- **Priority:** must have
- **User story:** As a household member, I want to invite others to my list
  so that we shop from one list.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - [ ] An invited person sees the list within a few seconds.

### FR-2: Mark an item bought

- **Priority:** must have
- **User story:** As a household member, I want to mark an item bought so
  that nobody else buys it.
- **Acceptance criteria:**
  - [ ] The item shows as bought for everyone.

### Non-functional requirements

- **NFR-1:** The list feels instant.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How measured |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Duplicate purchases | fewer | survey |

## Scope

- **In (this iteration):** sharing a list, marking items bought.
- **Out (explicitly excluded):** recipes.
- **Later:** price tracking.
