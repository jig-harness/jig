How the numbers are counted
class counts
Rows the filter keeps, per class, ignoring the class filter itself so another class can be chosen.
slots held
Launch records pending, alive or unknown on the account, over its cap. A takeover and its replaced record count once.
waited
From the hold’s beginning event to now, or to its ending event in the log.
duration
Launch to end of the record.
rounds
The ledger’s round at the end less its round at launch.
threads
Unresolved review threads, the first time a tick saw each round. Unseen rounds are gaps.
